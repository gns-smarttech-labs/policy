Privacy Policy — ScanContacts

Effective Date: September 01, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 01, 2026

Summary of Key Principles

Our app operates under an on-device processing architecture. We do not upload, transmit, or store your scanned business cards, images, or contact details on any server. All scanned data and contact information remain exclusively on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how ScanContacts ("we," "our," or "the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. On-Device Processing & Contact Data

The primary functionality of our App is to scan physical business cards (or card photos), extract text automatically, and allow you to edit, save, manage, call, or export contact details.

  • No Cloud OCR or Backend Processing: Text recognition and parsing are performed entirely on your device. No business card images or extracted text are ever transmitted to a server for processing.
  • No Cloud Storage or Backend Database: We do not operate any backend database to store your business cards or contact records.
  • Local Contacts Store: Saved contacts are written directly to your device's native address book.

3. Permissions & Device Access

To provide core offline scanning, contact management, and communication features, the App requests permission to access specific features on your device:

Permission Purpose in App Data Handling
Camera To take real-time photos of physical business cards for instant text extraction. Images are captured and processed live on-device only; camera feeds or photos are never uploaded off-device.
Photo Library / Gallery To let you select existing business card photos from your device gallery to scan or attach card images to contacts. Read locally during your active scanning or contact review session. Photos stay on your device.
Contacts Access To read existing saved contacts into your app list, save newly scanned contacts, update existing entries, and attach card photos. Read and written directly to your device's native phonebook. No contact data is sent to external servers.
Phone / Dialer To enable direct phone calls when tapping phone numbers on scanned card profiles or contact tiles. Initiates direct phone calls via your device's native phone dialer. We do not record or log calls.

4. Telemetry & Anonymous App Analytics

To ensure application stability, monitor scan extraction accuracy, and diagnose technical runtime errors, the App collects minimal, non-identifiable analytics.

What Is Transmitted:

  • Anonymous Scan Success Metrics: Categorical success indicators (e.g., whether a scan extracted both name and mobile, name only, mobile only, or failed) and raw line count metadata.
  • Scan Source: Whether a scan originated from the camera or gallery.
  • Basic Feature Usage: Anonymized user interaction events (e.g., opening the contact view screen, opening the new contact screen).
  • App Diagnostics & Errors: Crash and exception event context to help us fix bugs.

What Is NEVER Transmitted:

  • ❌ No names, email addresses, phone numbers, job titles, or company names extracted from business cards.
  • ❌ No card image files, camera snapshots, or photo attachments.
  • ❌ No personally identifiable user information (PII) or user contacts.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses a small number of privacy-conscious services to function:

  • On-device text recognition: Runs entirely on your device to read business card text.
  • Anonymous analytics and crash reporting: Receives the anonymous interaction events and error logs described in Section 4, provided by Google Firebase Analytics. Firebase operates under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • System-native launchers: Phone calls, emails, and web links are handled directly by your device's default dialer, email client, or browser.
  • CSV export & sharing: Exported contact files are saved to a temporary location on your device and shared only through your device's native share sheet, under your manual control.

6. Data Retention, Control, and Deletion

Because all scanned contact data resides exclusively on your local device:

  • Full Data Ownership: You retain complete control over all contact details and business card images.
  • Editing & Deletion: You can edit or delete any contact at any time through the App or via your device's native Contacts app.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the App removes app-specific cached files and temporary CSV exports. Contacts already saved to your device's native phonebook remain in your native Contacts app.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates, new features, or regulatory changes. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date."
